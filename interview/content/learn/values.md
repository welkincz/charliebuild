## Summary

Use these as flash cards. There is no model answer on purpose. A polished fictional story would be worse than a short real one you have not written yet.

## Key ideas

**Amazon, from the July 2024 notes.** Principles named: customer obsession, ownership, invent and simplify, deliver results, hire and develop the best. Prompts in those notes:
- Disagreed with a teammate
- Had to learn something quickly
- Made a simple solution for a complex problem
- Delivered under a tight deadline
- Helped someone grow

**Also in the Amazon hiring-manager notes:** a hard project, what you would redesign, a process you improved.

**Uber notes:** disagreed with a team member. Learned something quickly.

**Microsoft notes:** a production incident, a trade-off under pressure, driving a result across teams, learning a technology quickly. CI/CD depth is technical. Do not answer it with a slogan.

**Meta notes:** ownership, collaboration, stakeholders, and a light product-sense check.

**Netflix notes:** judgment, candor, and agency, in the company's own culture language. Speak to a decision you made and what you would do again.

## Interview angles

- The sourced behavioral item is a conflict question from an Amazon report. It stays in the practice bank with its grade and link.
- Other prompts above are Reported from your notes, not sourced.

## Pitfalls

- Memorizing someone else's story.
- Naming an employer, a customer, or a dollar figure you would not put in a public repo.
- Answering a technical CI/CD question with a leadership adjective.

## Say this in the interview

I pick the prompt, name the principle it is testing, and tell the story in STAR. If I do not have one yet, I leave a blank in my private note rather than invent a number.
