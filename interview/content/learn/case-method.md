## Summary

Meta's notes include a product-sense conversation beside SQL. You get a vague product and a short clock. Use a fixed outline so you do not improvise the structure.

## Key ideas

1. **Decision.** What will someone do differently if the metric moves?
2. **Users.** Who is in, who is out.
3. **Metric.** One primary, one guardrail. Definitions from the metrics page.
4. **Slices.** Two segments you would check before trusting the average.
5. **Experiment or analysis.** What comparison is fair? Watch for a population change.
6. **Data.** Which events you need. Stop. Do not design a warehouse unless they ask.

## Interview angles

- Practice on a generic marketplace or a content feed. The Meta notes use a game retention example. Do not present a personal banking initiative.

## Pitfalls

- Listing ten metrics.
- A roadmap of features with no measurement.
- Jumping to a streaming architecture.

## Say this in the interview

The decision is whether to keep the feature. The user is a new account in its first week. The primary metric is day-7 return, and the guardrail is the complaint rate. I would split by platform before I trusted the average.
