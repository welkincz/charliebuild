## Summary

The plan is eight core weeks at about ten hours each, plus four optional weeks. Meta is the primary loop: timed SQL and Python, then modeling and product sense. Amazon is second, including a leadership-principles round that uses STAR templates only. Coding reps run every week. Mocks start in week 5 as a Meta full loop plus Amazon leadership prompts. Amazon's technical loop stays a timed benchmark in week 6. System design at scale is the L5 stretch in the optional weeks. Banks are a safety appendix, not the study order.

## Key ideas

- **Daily habit, inside the ten hours.** Two SQL items and one Python or DSA item. Mark them done. Star anything you could not explain out loud.
- **Learn, then practice.** Each plan day links to the exact pages and questions. Do not collect extra tabs.
- **Core vs Stretch.** Core is the L4 baseline (SQL, grain, SCD, a batch pipeline, Meta-speed screens). Stretch is L5 (internals, exactly-once, orchestration depth, pipeline and lakehouse design at big-tech scale).
- **Origin labels.** Reported+sourced is the only set counted as asked at. Reported comes from your notes without a public source link. Worked example, Practice, and Self-check are study material.
- **Stories stay local.** Behavioral pages give templates and prompt lists. Write stories in a private note, not in this repo.

## Interview angles

- Can you state the grain before the query?
- Can you name the metric, the guardrail, and the cut of the data?
- After a mock, log one rubric row while the miss is fresh.

## Pitfalls

- Reading a page and calling it done. Done means you answered the self-check without looking, or you wrote the SQL.
- Saving stories in the repo or in a synced snippet. Keep them on the machine only.
- Leaving product sense until the end. Meta notes put a metric conversation on the onsite, so it starts in week 2.

## Say this in the interview

I study in a fixed order: concept, one worked pattern, then a timed attempt. I mark the item done only when I can restate the trade-off without the page open.
