## Summary

This page is intentionally empty of stories. Write them in a private note on your machine. A public repository is the wrong place for an employer, a title, a customer, or a result tied to your work.

## Key ideas

Use one block per story in that private note:

- **Id.** A word you can remember (migration, incident, mentor).
- **Prompts it can answer.** From the values page.
- **Situation.**
- **Task.**
- **Action.**
- **Result.**
- **What you would do differently.**

Eight to twelve stories cover a loop. Four is enough to start week 1. Map them to the principles yourself.

## Interview angles

- Week 1 of the plan tells you to draft the first stories. That work happens off this site.
- The mock behavioral round asks you to speak from that private note. The timer does not collect the text.

## Pitfalls

- Typing the story into the search box and assuming it stays local. Do not put it in this app at all.
- Using a sample you found in the old notes. Several of those were invented. Ignore them.

## Say this in the interview

I keep my stories in a local file that is not part of this project. I practice by reading the prompt here and answering out loud from memory.
